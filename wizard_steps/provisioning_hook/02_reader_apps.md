## Read in an app

A reader app can open the whole library as a catalog. It works with **KyBook 3**, **Yomu** and **KOReader**.

|||
### Catalog sign-in
- Catalog address: `{{ external_url }}/opds`
- Username: your email
- Password: the password you chose on the invite
|||

|||
### KOReader progress sync
- Open **Progress sync**, then **Custom sync server**.
- Server: `https://booksync.fromthecouch.com`
- Username: your email, with the same password.
- Never pick the built-in sync option.
|||

**Apple Books:** download an EPUB on the website and tap **Open in Books**.
